# Lab 002: Dense state-aware bounded-support isolation

## Narrow question

For which predeclared three-qubit state families and matched resource panels does a record-conditioned $R=2$ QR block circuit recover a useful fraction of a demonstrable full-system QR advantage over $R=1$?

## Motivation

Lab 001 qualifies the dense record encoder and QR seam only when a permitted block covers the full system. This lab enters the first nontrivial $N>R$ regime while retaining exact dense execution, so support, resource matching, state-family saturation, and learned record decoding can be separated before tensor-network approximation is introduced.

## Approval and scope

Human checkpoint `T-0006` approved the state-conditioned dense $N=3$, $R=2$ design. The executable contract is in [`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md). This lab excludes MPS, truncation, size transfer, spatial sharing, Hamiltonian heads, and $N>3$ work.

## Prospective evidence contract

The primary result is a per-state-family, per-resource-panel decision table. A family is informative only when the lower 95% confidence bound of $F_{\mathrm{full}}-F_{R=1}$ exceeds $0.05$. The normalized recovery score $q=(F_{R=2}-F_{R=1})/(F_{\mathrm{full}}-F_{R=1})$ is computed only then. Product-like, shallow locally entangled, and $W_3$ results remain separate; coefficient-matched and depth/gate-count-matched panels must be reported independently.

## Design

The approved plan freezes three state-family datasets, compares full/$R=2$/$R=1$/no-control under complementary coefficient- and depth/gate-count-matched panels, and advances through fixture, interface, numerical, one-step, analysis, oracle, and conditional multistep gates. Exact commands, budgets, schedules, tolerances, and stop rules are defined in [`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md).

## Methods and execution

Teacher-forced one-step cells reconstruct the normalized state history from each frozen initial state and measurement record. For each $t=1,\ldots,5$, the learned schedule receives the state after the adjacent $K_tK_{t-1}$ pair, targets the state before that pair, and conditions on $m_{t:}$. [`train_one_step_cell.py`](train_one_step_cell.py) runs exactly one family/panel/support/seed cell, enforces 128 Adam updates or an explicit budget failure, evaluates validation only, and saves both a checkpoint and a full training/result JSON. Frozen test data remain sealed until the validation matrix and analysis rules are locked.
