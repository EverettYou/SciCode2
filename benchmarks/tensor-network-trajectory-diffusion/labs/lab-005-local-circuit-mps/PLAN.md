# Lab 005: local QR-circuit MPS execution

## Objective

Replace the non-scalable global QR action by a brickwork circuit of local
two-qubit QR gates. Verify its MPS execution against dense states at $N=3$ and
then measure bond-dimension growth at larger $N$.

## Stable interfaces

`gate(seed, layer, bond) -> 4x4 unitary`; `brickwork(N, layer) -> bonds`; and
`apply_local_gate(MPS, gate, bond) -> MPS`.

## Steps

1. Generate seeded generic two-qubit QR gates shared by bond parity.
2. Apply identical brickwork layers to dense and MPS $N=3$ states.
3. Require maximum state discrepancy below $10^{-10}$.
4. Apply the circuit to product MPS states at larger $N$ with no truncation;
   record bond growth and execution resources.

## Completion criteria

Dense/MPS circuit parity passes at $N=3$ and the large-$N$ sweep records a
nontrivial realized bond dimension generated by the two-qubit gates.
