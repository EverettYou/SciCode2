# Decoding embedding research map

**Summary**: General-H compilation now has endpoint and rigorous early-time quantum original-error evaluation/compactness loops. Practical finite-anneal regimes, large standard-compiler coverage and calibrated difficulty remain open.

**Sources**: [General-H trial](../labs/lab-002-agent-test-general-h/REPORT.md), [structured follow-up](../labs/lab-003-surface-code-encoding-comparison/REPORT.md), [general-H closed-loop research](../labs/lab-004-general-h-evaluation-embedding-loop/REPORT.md).

**Last updated**: 2026-10-16.

## New finite-forward evidence

[Lab005](../labs/lab-005-general-h-annealing-relevance/REPORT.md) proves actual optimum-event early-time comparisons without a decoding oracle, including15/23 smaller balanced mappings and a limited3/24 standard extension with charged preprocessing. [Independent mapping audits](../labs/lab-005-general-h-annealing-relevance/results/independent_certified_mapping_audit.json) and [standard audit](../labs/lab-005-general-h-annealing-relevance/results/independent_standard_extension_audit.json) support the implementation. Guaranteed gains are tiny; balanced compilation costs about twice standard. The [standalone issue](../problem/ISSUE-general-h-finite-annealing.md) asks for an informative finite-anneal regime; its [creation assessment](../evaluation/ISSUE-ASSESSMENT-general-h-finite-annealing.md) does not establish acceptance.
