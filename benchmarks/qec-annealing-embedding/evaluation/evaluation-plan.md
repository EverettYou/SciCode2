# Intended scientific acceptance — formulation only

This record mirrors the question and supplies no gold answer or executed evaluator. The current create-benchmark workflow does not require a frozen evaluation plan; this file is retained for continuity.

A future result should connect three components: a general sparse-H compilation/embedding algorithm, an efficient evaluation procedure, and a substantive theoretical comparison or certified limitation under a stated annealing model. Assess mathematical fidelity, physical embedding witnesses, cost in sparse input and target size, reuse/update behavior, the assumptions and strength of theory, and reproducibility of chosen illustrations. A family-specific successful mapping alone does not meet the generalized objective.

No single chain statistic, standard embedding-validity proof or purely numerical search substitutes for the theoretical evaluation contribution. Conversely, no universal best embedding, all-input speedup or large faithful QPU simulation is required. Conditional results must identify their domain; uncertainty outside it is legitimate. Observable prediction is assessed only when O and the associated likelihood/hidden-outcome convention are supplied.

Methods, numerical budgets and example selection remain open at this authoring stage. Later experimental prompt design and formal calibration belong to their own requested workflows. No scientific experiment or model trial is authorized by this file.
