# Primary-source check during Lab005

Checked16 October2026. The following sources provide context, not priority evidence for the independently derived local implementation.

[Gyhm et al., Boltzmann Sampling by Diabatic Quantum Annealing, arXiv2409.18126v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.18126v4), SecsII/IV, derives a weak-coupling interaction-picture approximation with different response factors for fields and pair interactions. Its illustrative linear quench differs from this lab's fixed normalized forward schedule. It supports treating a Gibbs interpretation as a regime-dependent approximation, not an identity. Our finite-T expansion, affine-cost bound and explicit remainder are derived and checked locally; no novelty claim is made for Dyson/short-time reasoning.

[Amin, Searching for quantum speedup in quasistatic quantum annealers](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.04216) distinguishes a quasistatic frozen population from information about the underlying dynamics. Our terminal-equilibrium theorems do not establish equilibration or quantum speedup.

[Könz et al., Embedding Overhead Scaling of Optimization Problems in Quantum Annealing](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.2.040322) studies space/time overhead with simulated quantum annealing and fully connected source problems. Its numerical regime is not evidence for our general sparse-H real-time quantum model; its existence motivates explicit dynamics/resource accounting.

[Choi](https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.4884), [Cai et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2741), [Fang and Warburton](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.03291), and [D-Wave embedding guidance](https://docs.dwavequantum.com/en/latest/quantum_research/embedding_guidance.html) were rechecked for the issue's public starting references.
