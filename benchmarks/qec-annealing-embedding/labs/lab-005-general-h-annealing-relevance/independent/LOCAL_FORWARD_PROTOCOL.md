# Local-observable finite-forward remainder extension

Frozen after sparse moment/compact-selection results and before this extension. Investigate whether locality yields a useful rigorous finite-T bound without full-system norm scaling. Keep the same initial state, physical driver and schedule.

For a diagonal Walsh observable supported on s physical sites, bound nested commutators by local incident strength and support growth. With gE=max_q(|h_q|+Σ_r|J_qr|) and x=(1+gE)T<1, proposed even remainder beyond T^4 is [(1−x)^−s+(1+x)^−s]/2−1−binom(s+1,2)x²−binom(s+3,4)x⁴. Prove the time-ordered bound, independently test on exact small anchors, and use exact rational arithmetic for final inequalities.

Apply to unconditional original-error mean cost, a sum of single-root observables, for all48 ordinary fixed-coupling baseline/amplified pairs. Duration menu {.1,.05,.025,.0125,.00625,.003125,.0015625}; report the largest certified menu duration for an actual rounded mean-cost improvement, including all inconclusive cases. Validate on the independent ODE anchor readouts where available. No decoding oracle enters construction or evaluation.

These are local observable orderings, not an unconditional minimum-cost FEASIBLE-error probability ordering. Report this limitation prominently. Expected syndrome violations may be evaluated as a sum of root-support parity observables, but this extension does not silently replace the primary decoding event by average cost or average violations. This is adaptive reference research, not a new model-difficulty trial.
