# SciCode2 project thesis

**Summary**: SciCode2 should become a compounding research system in which papers seed benchmark questions, benchmark experiments produce durable evidence, and both global and local wikis make later scientific decisions cheaper and better grounded.

**Sources**: [Benchmark workspace](../benchmarks/README.md); [reusable skills](../skills/README.md)

**Last updated**: 2026-08-22

The project succeeds when it produces novel, workstation-feasible computational-research benchmarks with traceable evidence and complete research histories, not merely paper summaries or isolated coding exercises [Benchmark README](../benchmarks/README.md).

The global wiki supplies cross-paper and project knowledge. Each benchmark wiki remains the authority for its own evolving thesis and experiment record. Completed work should feed reusable knowledge upward without erasing the local provenance needed to audit a benchmark.

The strongest present support is now both architectural and operational: the paper inventory, benchmark lifecycle, local wiki protocol, lab loop, talk, and focused skills exist, and all currently fetched papers have been connected to reusable global themes [Paper catalog](../papers/papers.yml). The open question is whether later retrieval and benchmark design use these cross-paper distinctions selectively enough to improve research rather than merely grow the graph.
