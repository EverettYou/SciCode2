# SciCode2 project thesis

**Summary**: SciCode2 should compound research knowledge into achievable tasks whose scientific-judgment difficulty is measured by independent model trials, with durable reference evidence and transcripts supporting each proposal.

**Sources**: [Organizer requirements](../docs/scicode2/requirements.md); [benchmark workspace](../benchmarks/README.md); [reusable skills](../skills/README.md)

**Last updated**: 2026-10-07

The local research architecture aims for novel, workstation-feasible tasks with
traceable evidence and complete histories [Benchmark README](../benchmarks/README.md).
The organizer emails add the decisive measurement requirement: achievable tasks
must expose scientific reasoning failures in complete clean minimal and guided
runs. Research progress alone does not establish this difficulty
[Organizer prompt ladder](../docs/scicode2/requirements.md#deliverables-and-prompt-ladder).

The global wiki supplies cross-paper and project knowledge. Each benchmark wiki remains the authority for its own evolving thesis and experiment record. Completed work should feed reusable knowledge upward without erasing the local provenance needed to audit a benchmark.

The strongest present support is now both architectural and operational: the paper inventory, benchmark lifecycle, local wiki protocol, lab loop, talk, and focused skills exist, and all currently fetched papers have been connected to reusable global themes [Paper catalog](../papers/papers.yml). The open question is whether later retrieval and benchmark design use these cross-paper distinctions selectively enough to improve research rather than merely grow the graph.

The 2026-10-07 requirements ingest narrows the success criterion to include actual
clean-test evidence. The preparation, testing, and calibration protocols now exist,
but their existence does not demonstrate model failure, operational cross-client
automation, or improved candidate yield. These remain empirical questions
[Skill implementation boundary](../skills/README.md#proposal-testing-and-calibration).
