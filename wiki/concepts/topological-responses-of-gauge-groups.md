# Topological responses of gauge groups

**Summary**: Fractional topological response coefficients to coupled 0-form and 1-form backgrounds can distinguish global forms and symmetry-fractionalization variants that share the same local gauge algebra.

**Sources**: [Wan et al. (2024)](../../papers/wan2024trots/paper.pdf) [You and Wang (2022)](../../papers/you2022dqcag/paper.pdf)

**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

---

## Current synthesis

The Standard Model local algebra permits several global quotients $G_{SM,q}$ and additional fractionalization labels. The paper constructs baryon-minus-lepton-like $U(1)_{X_n}$ symmetries and a fractional response coupling their background to the magnetic 1-form background [Wan et al. (2024), §Abstract](../../papers/wan2024trots/paper.pdf#Abstract).

For selected $n$, the response coefficient can distinguish the quotient and fractionalization class; some single choices remain degenerate, while pairs such as $n=2,3$ separate the variants in the paper's table [Wan et al. (2024), §Sec.V-Table-II](../../papers/wan2024trots/paper.pdf#Sec.V-Table-II). This is a classification and response proposal, not an existing experimental measurement.

A complementary GUT construction uses a \(w_2w_3\) mixed gauge–gravitational anomaly of a Spin(10) Higgs sector to forbid a trivial symmetric region between competing Higgs phases. Its proposed parton QED is one anomaly-matching possibility rather than a response classification of Standard-Model quotient groups [You and Wang (2022), §III](../../papers/you2022dqcag/paper.pdf#page=5).
