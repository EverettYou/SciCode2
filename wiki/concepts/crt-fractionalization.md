# CRT fractionalization

**Summary**: Fermionic charge conjugation, reflection, and time reversal can form nontrivial extensions by fermion parity whose projective structure depends periodically on spacetime dimension and constrains symmetric masses.

**Sources**: [Wan et al. (2023)](../../papers/wan2023cffam/paper.pdf) [Li et al. (2024)](../../papers/li2024qmlcs/paper.pdf) [Li et al. (2026)](../../papers/li2026cfifh/paper.pdf)

**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

---

## Current synthesis

At first-quantized level, the groups generated by C, R, and T are extensions of their bosonic-looking quotient by fermion parity. For Dirac fermions the canonical structure has two possibilities with mod-8 dependence; Majorana and Weyl cases inherit dimension-dependent subgroups and available mass terms [Wan et al. (2023), §Sec.VII](../../papers/wan2023cffam/paper.pdf#Sec.VII).

The lattice many-body analysis promotes these symmetries to projective actions on the Hilbert space and interprets their anomalies as obstructions to symmetric gapping. It finds cancellation patterns consistent with eight staggered Majorana or four staggered Dirac copies under the stated internal-symmetry conditions, and constructs symmetry-preserving stabilizer interactions [Li et al. (2024), §Conclusions-and-discussions](../../papers/li2024qmlcs/paper.pdf#Conclusions-and-discussions).

The extended Hamiltonian treatment unifies ordinary and symplectic Majorana fields in real Clifford representations, classifies their CRT-internal groups with 8-fold Bott periodicity, and tracks symmetry actions on mass manifolds under domain-wall reduction. Dirac fields use complex Clifford representations, but their full CRT-internal groups still display an 8-fold pattern rather than merely inheriting complex Clifford 2-periodicity [Li et al. (2026), §I-IV](../../papers/li2026cfifh/paper.pdf#Sec.I-IV).

## Boundary

Fractionalization data, anomaly cancellation, and an explicit interacting gapping Hamiltonian are distinct layers. A group-extension table alone does not prove a realizable SMG path; a lattice construction alone does not classify all extensions.
