# Intrinsically gapless topological states

**Summary**: A critical state can carry anomaly-protected boundary structure even when no symmetry-preserving gapped phase realizes the same topology; in a scoped one-dimensional example, deconfined criticality supplies this intrinsically gapless topology.

**Sources**: [Yang et al. (2026)](../../papers/yang2026dcaig/paper.pdf)

**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

---

## Current synthesis

A gapless symmetry-protected topological state combines critical bulk correlations with boundary signatures protected by symmetry or anomaly. It is intrinsically gapless when those signatures have no symmetry-preserving gapped counterpart. In the two-leg spinless-fermion model studied by Yang et al., a line of deconfined critical points separates bond-density-wave and charge-density-wave phases, while a mixed anomaly enforces nontrivial boundary physics [Yang et al. (2026), §Introduction and Fig.1](../../papers/yang2026dcaig/paper.pdf#Introduction-and-Fig.1).

The reported DMRG evidence combines a (c\simeq1) conformal critical line, finite-entanglement and finite-size scaling, algebraically decaying boundary-bulk correlations, a twofold low-energy boundary sector, and an energy gap closing approximately as (1/L). Within the boundary doublet, layer exchange and layer fermion parity act as anticommuting Pauli operators, yielding a projective representation of the unbroken symmetry [Yang et al. (2026), §Numerical-results and SM §IV](../../papers/yang2026dcaig/paper.pdf#Numerical-results-and-SM.Sec.IV).

## Interpretation boundary

The result establishes a mechanism and lattice realization, not a universal equivalence between deconfined criticality and intrinsically gapless topology. The anomaly, preserved boundary symmetry, and accessible boundary sector must be checked for each model. Gapless edge modes with algebraic leakage into a critical bulk are also distinct from exponentially localized edge modes of a gapped SPT phase.

## Benchmark opportunities

- Reproduce the phase diagram, central charge, critical exponents, and boundary gap scaling with controlled DMRG convergence.
- Recover the projective boundary symmetry matrices from independently obtained low-energy states.
- Perturb the model by symmetry-preserving and symmetry-breaking terms to separate anomaly protection from fine tuning.
- Search for which one-dimensional DQCP symmetry classes do or do not admit intrinsically gapless topological interpretations.
