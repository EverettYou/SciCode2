# Grand-unified deconfined criticality

**Summary**: Competing Pati–Salam and Georgi–Glashow Higgs phases can be organized around an anomaly-constrained Spin(10) region whose candidate low-energy description contains fractionalized Higgs partons and an emergent gauge field.

**Sources**: [You and Wang (2022)](../../papers/you2022dqcag/paper.pdf)

**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

---

## Phase organization

Within a Spin(10) parent theory, condensation of Higgs fields in the 54 and 45 representations selects the Pati–Salam and Georgi–Glashow subgroups, while simultaneous condensation leaves their Standard-Model intersection. This recasts GUT vacua as neighboring zero-temperature phases connected by gauge-Higgs transitions rather than as a mandatory sequence reached only by raising energy [You and Wang (2022), §II](../../papers/you2022dqcag/paper.pdf#page=3).

The two Higgs order-parameter manifolds carry distinct point defects. A Wess–Zumino–Witten-like term intertwines their defects and realizes a mod-2 mixed gauge–gravitational anomaly represented in five dimensions by \(\exp[i\pi\int w_2w_3]\). Consequently, a fully symmetric Spin(10) region cannot be trivially gapped while preserving the assumed structure [You and Wang (2022), §III](../../papers/you2022dqcag/paper.pdf#page=5).

## Candidate anomaly matching

One proposed realization fractionalizes the Higgs field into ten Dirac fermionic partons coupled to an emergent \(U(1)'\) gauge field. In 3+1 dimensions the deconfined gauge phase can occupy a finite region rather than only a point, with color–flavor-separated partons and a decoupled gauge boson [You and Wang (2022), §III](../../papers/you2022dqcag/paper.pdf#page=7).

The anomaly constrains the infrared but does not uniquely select this QED description; another CFT or topological order could match it. The phase diagram, dark radiation, and early-universe consequences are theoretical proposals, not experimental evidence. The review also deliberately suppresses spacetime-symmetry details and electroweak Higgs dynamics [You and Wang (2022), §§I and IV](../../papers/you2022dqcag/paper.pdf#page=2).

## Benchmark hooks

- Verify subgroup intersections and Higgs stabilizers directly from matrix representations.
- Compute the defect homotopy groups and reproduce the mod-2 linking phase.
- Check anomaly matching of the proposed parton representation through independent characteristic-class calculations.
- Enumerate alternative anomaly-matching infrared theories and distinguish their observable consequences.
