**Summary**: Builds a symmetry-complete hot-spot RG for RTG and relates displacement-field-dependent nesting to competing metallic and pairing instabilities.

## Scientific role

The paper contributes both a material-specific RTG phase analysis and a reusable computational representation of one-loop hot-spot RG in a symmetry-reduced Majorana interaction basis [Lu et al. (2022), §§III–V](../../papers/lu2022cmaus/paper.pdf#page=4).
