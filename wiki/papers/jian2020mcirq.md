# Measurement-induced criticality in random quantum circuits

**Summary**: Maps measurement-induced entanglement criticality in Haar-random circuits to replica statistical mechanics and solves its infinite-local-dimension percolation limit.

**Sources**: [Jian et al. (2020)](../../papers/jian2020mcirq/paper.pdf)

**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Scientific role

The paper supplies a replica mapping for trajectory-averaged Rényi quantities, conformal scaling predictions, and an exact (d=\infty) percolation solution while identifying finite (d) as a distinct open universality problem [Jian et al. (2020), §§III–VII](../../papers/jian2020mcirq/paper.pdf#page=5).
