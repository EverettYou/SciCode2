# Topological and symmetry-enriched random quantum critical points

**Summary**: Shows that one-dimensional infinite-randomness fixed points can carry distinct symmetry-enriched boundary and nonlocal structure despite identical local bulk scaling.

**Sources**: [Duque et al. (2021)](../../papers/duque2021tasrq/paper.pdf)

**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Scientific role

The paper combines strong-disorder RG, topology, SBRG, DMRG, and Floquet constructions to establish topologically distinct random Ising critical points and random-singlet phases [Duque et al. (2021), main text](../../papers/duque2021tasrq/paper.pdf#page=1).
