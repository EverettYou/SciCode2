# Variational Monte Carlo study of symmetric mass generation in a bilayer honeycomb lattice model

**Summary**: Uses a parton-guided Slater–Jastrow ansatz to study direct SMG and expose a gauge-charged variational mass behind a symmetry-restored physical state.

**Sources**: [Hou and You (2023)](../../papers/hou2023vmcso/paper.pdf)

**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Scientific role

The paper provides a variational construction of bilayer-honeycomb SMG and reports \(J_c=3.48\), \(\nu=0.96(3)\), and physical-fermion scaling dimension \(\Delta_c=1.31(4)\). It interprets the enlarged dimension and optimized parton mass as support for an fDQCP description [Hou and You (2023), §§III–V](../../papers/hou2023vmcso/paper.pdf#page=3).
