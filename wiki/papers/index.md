# Papers

**Summary**: Papers are immutable scientific inputs and benchmark seeds; the catalog owns publication identity, paper folders own fetched assets and assessments, and the global wiki compiles reusable knowledge across them.

**Sources**: [Paper catalog](../../papers/papers.yml)

**Last updated**: 2026-08-22

The canonical paper inventory is [papers/papers.yml](../../papers/papers.yml). A paper moves from listed to fetched, assessed, and manually reviewed. Its `assessment.yml` records benchmark-oriented judgment, follow-up ideas, and pitfalls without becoming a substitute for the paper itself [Paper catalog](../../papers/papers.yml).

Global ingest should update shared concept, method, model, comparison, implementation, and question pages rather than create one summary page per paper. Paper-specific facts remain traceable to the local PDF or source archive. Reviewed assessments guide selection but are not scientific evidence.
