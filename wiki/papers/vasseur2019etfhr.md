# Entanglement transitions from holographic random tensor networks

**Summary**: Maps random-tensor-network entanglement transitions to bulk domain-wall ordering and studies how fluctuating geometry changes critical and entanglement scaling.

## Scientific role

The paper establishes an analytically tractable ensemble model for entanglement-scaling transitions and connects regular and fluctuating tensor-network geometries to statistical mechanics and quantum gravity [Vasseur et al. (2019), §§II–VI](../../papers/vasseur2019etfhr/paper.pdf#page=3).
