## Scientific role

The paper turns Green-function zeros into a concrete Fermi-surface SMG diagnostic through Luttinger-volume arguments, cluster perturbation, fluctuating-order field theory, and a proposed spectroscopy reconstruction [Lu et al. (2023), §§II–IV](../../papers/lu2023gfzif/paper.pdf#page=2).
