## Scientific role

The paper provides a symmetry-organized weak-coupling bridge among tBLG pocket geometry, valley fluctuations, intervalley pairing, IVCW order, and Hund-selected singlet/triplet sectors [You and Vishwanath (2019), §§I–III](../../papers/you2019sfvfa/paper.pdf#page=2).
