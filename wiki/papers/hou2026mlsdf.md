## Scientific role

The paper provides a trajectory-level method for jointly learning conserved quantities, symmetry dimension, and Lie-algebra structure, validated on Kepler (so(4)) and oscillator (su(3)) benchmarks [Hou et al. (2026), §Abstract and III](../../papers/hou2026mlsdf/paper.pdf#Abstract-and-Sec.III).
