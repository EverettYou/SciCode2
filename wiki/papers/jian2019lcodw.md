# Lattice construction of duality with non-Abelian gauge fields in 2+1D

**Summary**: Exactly maps an interacting (SO(3)) gauge–boson–Majorana lattice path integral to a free Majorana theory and relates the construction to a proposed infrared duality.

**Sources**: [Jian et al. (2019)](../../papers/jian2019lcodw/paper.pdf)

## Scientific role

The paper extends exact Euclidean lattice duality constructions from Abelian to orthogonal non-Abelian gauge groups and makes operator and symmetry correspondences explicit [Jian et al. (2019), §§II–IV](../../papers/jian2019lcodw/paper.pdf#page=2).
