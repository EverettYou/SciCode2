# Gradient-based Wang–Landau algorithm: A novel sampler for output distribution of neural networks over the input space

**Summary**: Transfers density-of-states sampling to neural-network output landscapes and uses model gradients to explore rare output regions.

**Sources**: [Liu et al. (2023)](../../papers/liu2023gwaan/paper.pdf)

**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Scientific role

The paper introduces gradient-based Wang–Landau sampling for estimating a classifier's output histogram over a finite discrete input domain, including rare output bins that uniform or random local proposals visit inefficiently [Liu et al. (2023), §§3–5](../../papers/liu2023gwaan/paper.pdf#page=3).
