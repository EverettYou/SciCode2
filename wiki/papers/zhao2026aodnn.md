## Scientific role

The paper supplies an explicit action-only learning objective for bijective coarse-graining and tests it against analytic Gaussian decimation and the two-dimensional \(\phi^4\) critical flow [Zhao et al. (2026), §§II–IV](../../papers/zhao2026aodnn/paper.pdf#page=3). Its note-added gradient caveat is part of the reusable validation boundary, not an implementation footnote [Zhao et al. (2026), §V](../../papers/zhao2026aodnn/paper.pdf#page=10).
