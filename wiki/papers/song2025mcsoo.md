**Summary**: Maps ensemble-averaged dual-unitary operator spreading to a classical Markov process and samples density and operator-entanglement transitions at polynomial cost.

## Scientific role

The source provides a scalable support-space simulation and identifies where simple mean field succeeds, where correlations invalidate it, and how subsystem geometry controls an operator-entanglement transition [Song et al. (2025), §§2–4](../../papers/song2025mcsoo/paper.pdf#page=3).
