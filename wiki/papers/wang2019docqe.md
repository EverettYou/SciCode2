## Scientific role

The paper provides dynamical numerical evidence for a deconfined algebraic spin liquid and its confinement into a VBS, with explicit spectral signatures and analytic-continuation limitations [Wang et al. (2019), §§II–V](../../papers/wang2019docqe/paper.pdf#page=3).
