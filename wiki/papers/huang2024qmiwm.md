## Scientific role

The paper supplies a constructive route from Wannier-obstructed electron bands to local effective spin models and identifies the overlap-controlled regime where flat-band ferromagnetism gives way to frustrated magnetism [Huang et al. (2024), §§II–VI](../../papers/huang2024qmiwm/paper.pdf#page=2).
