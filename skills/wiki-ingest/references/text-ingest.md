# Text-source ingest

Use for papers, assessments, documentation, and other primarily textual sources. In benchmark scope these normally live under `problem/papers/`; in global scope they retain canonical project-root-relative locations such as `papers/`, `skills/`, `talk/`, or `benchmarks/`.

Read the complete source or the complete scientifically relevant portion. Extract:

- concepts, definitions, assumptions, and scope;
- claims and their evidence, uncertainty, and locators;
- methods described at the scientific level;
- limitations, contradictions, followups, and pitfalls;
- relationships to existing wiki concepts, implementations, labs, and thesis claims.

Distinguish published claims from assessment suggestions and new wiki inference. For project self-knowledge, summarize ownership and relationships and link to the resource that owns operational detail. Cite PDFs by page/section/figure and source text by heading or line when stable. Update implementation pages when a paper clarifies what code is intended to compute, but do not infer that a repository faithfully implements the paper without code evidence.
