# Query outputs for research decisions

## Research idea

Return:

1. concise idea or question;
2. wiki evidence and raw-source provenance;
3. parent or competing ideas;
4. why it has information value;
5. assumptions and likely pitfalls;
6. the cheapest observation that could support or prune it;
7. recommended `wiki/ideas/` track and next action.

## Next lab

Return:

1. motivation tied to a wiki uncertainty;
2. hypothesis and meaningful alternatives;
3. minimal discriminating design;
4. stable `src/` interfaces to import;
5. observables, controls, uncertainty, and invariants;
6. informative success and failure outcomes;
7. expected result files and wiki-ingest handoff;
8. stop or redesign conditions.

Do not optimize for a positive result. Optimize for changing the posterior over important ideas.
